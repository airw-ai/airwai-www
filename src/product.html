How it works

From the field to your system of record, automatically.

01 · DRIVE

Mount the rig and drive the route at normal operating speed.

02 · CAPTURE

Survey-grade LiDAR and a global-shutter camera record every surface.

03 · DETERMINE

LAIRA turns raw capture into condition findings, with no manual scoring.

04 · PUBLISH

Findings land in Esri, the mapping system your agency already runs.

Accountable by design

A human still signs. Every time.

LAIRA stages the finding, the determination, and the exact NOTAM wording. A qualified person reviews and approves. The model does the work; the accountable human keeps the pen.
