Logo

The wordmark

The AIRWAI wordmark is the primary mark. Ink on light backgrounds; the chalk (white) wordmark on dark Federal-Navy heros and bands.

Safe area

Keep clearance equal to the letterform height on every side. Never crowd the mark with text or graphics.

Don't recolor

Ink-on-light or chalk-on-navy only. No gradients, no off-brand fills, no drop shadows.

Don't distort

Preserve the aspect ratio. Scale proportionally; never stretch, condense, or rotate.

Contrast

Place only on backgrounds that hold legibility. On busy imagery, use a solid navy or chalk field behind the mark.

Voice

How Airwai sounds

Tagline, fixed wording and punctuation: Airwai: the autonomous civil engineer.

Confident, not boastful

State what it does.

"Real-time FOD detection on every operator drive." Not "revolutionary AI that changes everything."

Specific, not buzzwordy

Name the standard.

Cite the standard (ASTM D5340, UFC 3-260), the sensor, the accuracy (2–10 cm RTK). Specifics earn trust with engineers.

Operational, not aspirational

Describe what runs today.

What the system does now, with customers already running it — not what it will do someday.
