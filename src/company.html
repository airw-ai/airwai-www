About Airwai

The infrastructure holds the country up. We keep it read.

Every runway, road, and airfield in the country still gets inspected by someone walking it with a clipboard, weeks behind. Airwai builds the software that reads infrastructure automatically, turning an ordinary drive into audit-ready engineering decisions, so the people who keep it safe spend their day on judgment.

Why we exist

The mandate to inspect keeps growing. The teams that carry it do not. We close that gap with software, not headcount, so a shrinking workforce can hold a rising standard.

What we believe

Four things we won’t compromise.

01

The record has to hold up.

Compliance is the product, not a feature bolted on. Every finding is geolocated, time-stamped, and scored to the standard the inspector already answers to, so the record survives an audit years later.

02

A qualified person signs.

The software does the reading; a licensed engineer owns the decision. We deliver engineering outcomes for a human to approve, never a black-box score that no one will put their name to.

03

Augment the engineer.

Automation gives field days back, not pink slips. The hours LAIRA saves on data collection become billable engineering work, so teams win more of it with the people they already have.

04

Built for secure ground.

We build for military installations and critical infrastructure from the start: NDAA-compliant hardware, SAM-registered, data that stays where it belongs. Security is a precondition, not an upgrade.
