Thesis

hold up.

Airwai is a software platform. The intelligence is the determination engine that decides what a field condition means and files the record; the Rig is one of three ways data comes in, and only the Rig certifies. Every output cites its standard. Every claim terminates at a public source. The result is a record you can hand an auditor without a second thought.

We sell defensible outcomes, not sensors. Federal contracting officers, state DOT engineers, and AEC primes don’t buy a model. They buy the discharge of an obligation they cannot skip: a citation-backed record that survives the audit and stands up against a certificate action.

Security posture

Built for secure sites
from the ground up.

The mission

Intelligence for the
infrastructure that matters.

We operate at airfields, military installations, and roadway networks that carry national-level operations. The condition of that infrastructure is the record the FAA and the Department of War hold operators to. Today it lives in a binder.

Department of War installations alone manage thousands of miles of runway, taxiway, apron, and access pavement. The current default is hand-walked inspections on a slow cadence, paper records, and reactive maintenance. We’re building the platform that turns continuous-scan data into decisions, in the workflow operators already use.

The same pattern carries through to the roughly 520 FAA Part 139 airports that owe a daily self-inspection (14 CFR 139.327) and condition reporting (139.339), with 277 now standing up a Subpart E Safety Management System; to state DOTs; and to the AEC primes on the hook for a survey-grade baseline. One platform, sensor-agnostic, citation-chained, audit-ready by construction.
