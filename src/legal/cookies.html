How we use cookies

Airwai uses cookies and related technologies on airwai.com to operate the site and understand traffic. We aim to minimize cookies; our primary analytics tool (Cloudflare Web Analytics) is cookieless by design.

Categories of cookies

  • Strictly necessary — required for the site to function (e.g., load balancing, security against bot traffic via Cloudflare WAF). These cannot be disabled.
  • Functional — remember your preferences within a session. Currently minimal use.
  • Analytics — measure traffic patterns. Cloudflare Web Analytics is cookieless. Where additional analytics tools are enabled, they may set cookies.
  • Marketing — currently not in use on this site.
