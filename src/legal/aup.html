Acceptable Use Policy.

Version: aup-v1 · Effective Date: pending

Scope

This Acceptable Use Policy applies to everyone who uses the LAIRA platform, Airwai's pavement and airfield condition software. It is incorporated into the Self-Serve Terms of Service and any master agreement.

Data you may not submit

LAIRA is not designed for personal or special-category data. Do not submit:

  • health information, payment-card data, biometric identifiers, or government-issued identifiers;
  • data you are not authorized to share, or that infringes a third party's rights;
  • classified information or data subject to export controls you are not cleared to transfer.

Field imagery can incidentally capture people, vehicles, or license plates. That incidental capture is expected; deliberately submitting the categories above is not permitted.

Conduct you may not engage in

  • Probing, scanning, or testing the vulnerability of the platform without prior written authorization, or breaching any security or authentication measure.
  • Interfering with or disrupting the service, or imposing an unreasonable load on it.
  • Reverse-engineering the platform or attempting to extract labels, model weights, or scoring internals.
  • Reselling, sublicensing, or providing the service to a third party except as your agreement allows.
  • Removing, obscuring, or misrepresenting the Screening — not for compliance use notice, or presenting a screening output as a certified result.
  • Using the service to violate any law or the rights of others.

Your responsibilities

You are responsible for your account, your authorized users, the accuracy of the data you submit, and maintaining your own ArcGIS entitlements and authorizations. You are responsible for deciding whether an outcome is appropriate for your use, subject to the screening boundary.

Suspension

Airwai may suspend or limit access to address a security risk, a violation of this policy, non-payment, or a legal requirement. Where practical, Airwai will give notice and an opportunity to cure.

Reporting abuse or a vulnerability

Report abuse or a suspected security vulnerability to info@airwai.com. Airwai supports good-faith security research and will not pursue action against research conducted in line with a coordinated-disclosure approach. (A dedicated security@ reporting channel is being provisioned.)
