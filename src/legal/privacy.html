Overview

Airwai, Inc. ("Airwai," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, share, and protect information when you visit airwai.com, request a pilot, or otherwise interact with us. It applies to public-facing interactions only; product-side data (the LAIRA platform and APIs) is governed by your contract with Airwai.

Information we collect

We collect information in three ways:

  • Information you provide directly — name, organization, role, email, phone number, and the surface or procurement vehicle you're interested in, submitted via the pilot-scope form on /pricing/ or any email to info@airwai.com or gov@airwai.com.
  • Information collected automatically — browser type, IP address, pages visited, referring URL, and similar usage data via Cloudflare Web Analytics (cookieless by default) and any other analytics tools enabled on this site.
  • Information from service providers — HubSpot CRM (form submissions), Google Workspace (email correspondence), and AWS (product infrastructure metadata when applicable).

How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiries and schedule pilots
  • To improve our website and services
  • To meet legal, regulatory, and federal contracting requirements (including SBIR, FAR, and DoW acquisition rules)
  • To send relevant communications about Airwai products, events, or partnerships you may have opted into

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information with:

  • Service providers under contract — HubSpot, AWS, Cloudflare, Google Workspace — strictly for the purposes of operating this website and our business
  • As required by law or government contract — federal acquisition reps, court orders, audit requests
  • In a business transition — acquisition, merger, or sale of assets

Your rights — California (CPRA) and similar jurisdictions

California residents may request access, deletion, correction, or portability of personal information we hold about them, in accordance with the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA). To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below. We will respond within 45 days, with a one-time extension where the law permits.

Data security

We use commercially reasonable safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Our infrastructure runs on AWS and Cloudflare with industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify affected individuals and regulators as required by law in the event of a breach.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. We will post material changes prominently on this site.
