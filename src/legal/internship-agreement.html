Legal · Airwai Applied AI Internship

Internship Agreement.

Version: internship-agreement-v1 · Effective: 31 October 2026 · Party: Airwai, Inc.

This Internship Agreement (“Agreement”) is between Airwai, Inc., a Delaware corporation, 13214 Crenshaw Blvd., Gardena, CA 90249, USA (“Airwai”), and the person who accepts it by checking the agreement box on Airwai’s application form and typing their full name, or by signing below (the “Intern”).

1. The Internship. Airwai runs a remote, three-month applied-AI internship (the “Internship”). Airwai assigns the Intern’s tasks, such as reviewing, labeling and correcting images and model outputs used to train Airwai’s computer-vision models, along with related learning and project work. Tasks may change during the Internship. Applying does not guarantee a place. Airwai confirms admission, the start date and the Intern’s supervisor by email.

2. Educational Internship; No Employment. The Internship is an unpaid educational internship, designed to count toward the Intern’s university studies. It does not create an employment relationship, and the Intern receives no salary, wages or benefits. Airwai provides training, supervision, accounts and tools, and regular feedback. When the Intern completes the Internship, Airwai issues a certificate and, if the Intern’s university asks, confirms the Intern’s participation and evaluation for academic credit. The Intern remains responsible for meeting their university’s own requirements. Any paid role would require a separate written agreement.

3. Term. The Internship lasts three months from the start date, unless the parties extend it by email. Either party may end it earlier at any time by written notice, including by email.

4. Responsibilities. The Intern will take part in good faith in the tasks Airwai assigns, follow Airwai’s guidelines and instructions, communicate through Airwai’s channels, and treat colleagues with respect.

5. Confidential Information. “Confidential Information” means any non-public information Airwai, or anyone acting for Airwai, makes available to the Intern, in any form, that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential. It includes imagery and video, annotations and labels, datasets, model outputs, model weights, software, tools and their access credentials, labeling guidelines, the names and locations of customer sites, customer and partner identities, and Airwai’s business, technical and financial information. It does not include information that: (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the Intern; (b) the Intern already knew, free of any duty of confidentiality; (c) a third party lawfully provides to the Intern without restriction; or (d) the Intern develops independently, outside the Internship, without using Airwai’s Confidential Information.

6. Use and Protection. The Intern will:

  • (a) use Confidential Information only to do Internship work;
  • (b) access it only through the accounts and tools Airwai provides, and keep those credentials private;
  • (c) not download, copy, screenshot, photograph or record it, or store it on a personal device or personal cloud account, unless Airwai allows it in writing;
  • (d) not upload or paste it into any outside service, including public AI tools, chat assistants, code repositories or social media;
  • (e) not share it with anyone, including classmates, university staff or other interns outside Airwai’s own channels, except as Airwai directs; and
  • (f) tell Airwai promptly at info@airwai.com if any Confidential Information is lost, exposed or used without permission.

The Intern may disclose Confidential Information if the law or a court requires it, provided the Intern gives Airwai prompt notice where lawful, so Airwai can seek protection, and discloses only what is required.

7. Ownership of Work; No License.

  • (a) Everything the Intern creates in the Internship belongs to Airwai, including annotations, labels, corrections, datasets, code, documents, reports, presentations, ideas and improvements (the “Work”). The Intern transfers to Airwai, now and as each item is created, all economic rights in the Work, including copyright and any other intellectual property rights, worldwide, for their full term. Where the law does not allow a right to be transferred, the Intern grants Airwai an exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, worldwide license to use it in any way and agrees not to assert it against Airwai or its customers.
  • (b) To the extent the law allows, the Intern agrees not to exercise any moral rights in the Work against Airwai, and agrees Airwai need not name the Intern as its author.
  • (c) The Intern will sign any further document Airwai reasonably requests to confirm these rights.
  • (d) This Agreement gives the Intern no license or rights in any of Airwai’s Confidential Information or intellectual property.
  • (e) The Intern may describe their role, skills and general tasks in a CV, portfolio or university report, and may present final Internship work to their university, as long as no Confidential Information is shown. Airwai reviews any presentation or report that includes Internship material before it is shared.

8. Return or Deletion. When the Internship ends, or earlier if Airwai asks, the Intern will promptly return or delete all Confidential Information and Work in their possession and, if asked, confirm this in writing. Airwai closes the Intern’s access to its tools at the same time.

9. The Intern’s Personal Data. Airwai uses the Intern’s name, contact details, university and Internship records to run the Internship, confirm participation to the Intern’s university, and issue the certificate. Airwai shares them with the Intern’s university only for those purposes, keeps them only as long as needed for them and for legal record-keeping, and stores them with its service providers, which may be in the United States. The Intern may ask to see, correct or delete their data at info@airwai.com, in line with applicable data-protection law.

10. Survival. The confidentiality obligations last for five (5) years after the relevant Confidential Information was first disclosed. Sections 7 and 8 survive without time limit.

11. General. This Agreement is the entire understanding between the parties on its subject and supersedes prior discussions of it. It may be amended only in a writing signed or electronically accepted by both parties, and no failure to enforce a provision is a waiver. Airwai may assign this Agreement to an affiliate or to a successor by merger or sale of substantially all of its assets; the Intern may not assign it. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Albania, and the competent courts of Tirana, Albania, decide any dispute. A breach may cause Airwai harm that money cannot repair, so Airwai may also seek interim or injunctive measures. If any provision is unenforceable, the rest remains in effect. The Intern’s electronic acceptance (checking the agreement box and typing their full name) has the same effect as a handwritten signature. This Agreement is written in English; if Airwai provides a translation, the English text governs.
