What screening is

LAIRA Screening is a fast, first-pass condition read of your pavement, produced from imagery or video and published as layers into the ArcGIS environment your organization already runs. It is built to give you an early, useful picture of pavement condition in days, without a procurement cycle.

What screening is not

Screening is not a certified inspection and is not for compliance use. It does not satisfy, replace, or discharge any legal, regulatory, or contractual obligation. In particular, screening does not constitute or substitute for a Federal Aviation Administration Part 139 self-inspection (14 CFR Part 139), a Pavement Condition Index survey performed to a certifying standard, or any engineering determination that a licensed professional must sign. Do not rely on screening outputs for a safety-of-life decision, a regulatory filing, or a certification.

The label rides every layer

Every layer and every report we deliver carries the notice Screening — not for compliance use. That notice is part of the product by design, and you agree not to remove, obscure, or misrepresent it.

Where the certified path begins

When you need defensible, certifiable evidence, that is the Rig — Airwai's compliance-grade tier, quoted to your scope and delivered with the sensing and process controls a certification requires. Screening is the on-ramp; the Rig certifies. If your need is compliance-grade, tell us and we will route you to the Rig rather than sell screening as a substitute.

Outcomes, not labels

Screening returns outcomes — condition layers and reports — through the platform and the API. It does not return raw labels, model weights, or scoring internals, and those outcomes are not a certified determination.
