A path forward

Strong contributors may be offered paid work as Airwai Albania grows.

The survey drives itself. You teach it what it sees.

One pass at operating speed becomes thousands of frames. Your labels are what turn those frames into a working model.

Real work, not a class exercise

What you label this week trains next week's model.

This is production machine-learning data work. You use CVAT, the browser-based annotation platform the team runs, and work alongside an Airwai engineer who reviews your batches and gives feedback every week.

What you'll work on

Three projects, all pavement.

Pavement is the model Airwai most needs to build out, so that is where your work goes. You are assigned to, or rotate across, the projects below; each has its own label taxonomy and guidelines document.

Project 01

Airfield pavement

Trace cracks, spalling, joint and slab damage on runways and taxiways so the model learns to find and grade them.

Polygons · polylines
Project 02

Roadway pavement

Outline cracks, potholes, and patches in road imagery, where lighting, lane paint, and surfaces change the problem.

Polygons · polylines
Project 03

Model review & correction

Correct what the pavement model predicted. It is the fastest path to a better model, and a window into how one learns.

Review · QA

The model drafts; you make it right. Our current model labels every image first, so you review and correct its work instead of drawing from scratch. A hundred images that once took half a day now take about fifteen minutes for an experienced annotator. After a month of ramp-up, interns work toward about 1,000 corrected images a week, with quality checked before speed.

The six months

Month by month, closer to the model.

  1. Month 1

    Onboard

    Learn the labeling platform, the guidelines, and your first project.

  2. Month 2

    Produce

    Reach the full weekly target with quality checked every week.

  3. Month 3

    Annotate

    Label pavement imagery across airfield and roadway sets, and help train the models.

  4. Month 4

    Review

    Audit other interns' work against reference images.

  5. Month 5

    Improve the model

    Study the model's worst mistakes and turn them into training data.

  6. Month 6

    Present

    Write up what you found and present it to the team.
