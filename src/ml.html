Real work, not a class exercise

What you label this week trains next week's model.

This is production machine-learning data work. You use CVAT, the browser-based annotation platform the team runs, and work alongside an Airwai engineer who reviews your batches and gives feedback every week.

What you'll work on

Three projects, one engine.

You are assigned to, or rotate across, the projects below. Each has its own label taxonomy and a guidelines document.

Project 01

Pavement & airfield distress

Trace cracks, potholes, spalling, and joint damage in road and runway imagery so the model learns to find and grade them.

Polygons · polylines
Project 02

Camera event detection

Box vehicles, people, aircraft, wildlife, and debris (FOD) in fixed-camera footage that trains real-time alerting.

Bounding boxes · tracks
Project 03

Model review & correction

Correct what the current model predicted. It is the fastest path to a better model, and a window into how one learns.

Review · QA

Your pace follows the project. Detailed pavement work runs slower than boxing camera frames, so your lead sets the target when you are assigned. There is no blanket quota to chase.
