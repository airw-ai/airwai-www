What LAIRA Rig does not do.

Stated upfront, before the buyer asks. Borrowed from the D6433 matrix §9.

  • 01 Not a certified Class-1 inertial profiler today. AASHTO R 56 native certification is in active development. The Rig accepts your existing Greenwood, AMES, or ICC profiler IRI with cert chain preserved.
  • 02 Not a Falling Weight Deflectometer. Subsurface structural evaluation requires physical loading.
  • 03 Not a friction tester. Friction requires tire contact.
  • 04 Retroreflectivity is a calibrated screening estimate. Compliance audits still need handheld ASTM E1710 instrument readings.
