## How we use cookies

Airwai uses cookies and related technologies on airwai.com to:

- Operate the site (functional / strictly necessary)
- Remember your preferences
- Measure traffic via **Cloudflare Web Analytics** (cookieless by default)

## Third-party services in use

| Service | Purpose |
|:---|:---|
| Cloudflare | DNS, CDN, WAF, web analytics (cookieless) |
| HubSpot | CRM and form submissions on the pricing page |
| Google Workspace | Email and document collaboration (no cookies on this site) |
| AWS | Product infrastructure (no cookies on this site) |

## Managing cookies

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies via their settings. Doing so may break functional aspects of the site (such as form submissions).

California residents (CPRA) may submit opt-out requests for sale or sharing of personal information at [info@airwai.com](mailto:info@airwai.com).

## Updates

This policy is updated as our cookie practices change. The "Last Updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
