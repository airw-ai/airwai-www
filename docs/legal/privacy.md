## Overview

Airwai, Inc. ("Airwai," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, share, and protect information when you visit airwai.com, request a demo, or otherwise interact with us.

## Information we collect

- Information you provide directly (e.g., name, organization, email, role) when requesting a pilot or contacting us
- Information collected automatically (browser type, IP, pages visited) via Cloudflare Web Analytics and similar tools
- Information from service providers (HubSpot CRM, Google Workspace, AWS)

## How we use information

- To respond to your inquiries and schedule pilots
- To improve our website and services
- To meet legal, regulatory, and federal contracting requirements

## Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information with service providers under contract (HubSpot, AWS, Cloudflare, Google) and as required by law or government contract.
