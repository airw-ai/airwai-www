## Voice — six principles

1. **Confident, not boastful** — "Real-time edge inference under 200 ms"
2. **Specific, not buzzwordy** — name standards, sensors, resolutions
3. **Operational, not aspirational** — describe today's capabilities
4. **Contrarian when earned** — concrete comparisons, never name-calling
5. **Citation as posture** — inline links show the work
6. **Dry humor permitted** — engineers value substance over fluff

## What we are NOT

Four explicit refusals:

1. **Not a generic AI startup** — no cyan/purple gradients, neural-net visuals, or "AI-powered" framing
2. **Not a hardware company** — sensor is one of three ingest tiers, never headline
3. **Not friendly SaaS** — no mascots, rounded illustrations, or smiling-team photography
4. **Not civil-engineering legacy** — no hard-hat yellow, blueprint cyan, or safety-cone iconography

## Notable brand positions

- **"Severity is not the point. Decisiveness is."** — core brand distinction
- **"The dashboard IS the brand."** — product surface as visual identity
- **"Citation as posture"** — trust built through inline sourcing
- **"Sensor-agnostic platform"** — technical positioning statement
- **"The 5% earns its keep"** — accent color restraint principle
