Certifications and compliance posture

What we hold,
what is in progress.

Stated honestly: what we hold, what is in progress, and what is inherited from our infrastructure.

  • SOC 2 Type II: in progress. Airwai is engaged in a SOC 2 Type II audit process. We do not yet hold the report. When it issues, it will be available here under NDA, and this page will be updated the day it is.
  • NIST SP 800-171: alignment in progress. Our security program is built against the NIST SP 800-171 control families. This alignment is currently self-assessed, not third-party certified, and we describe it that way in our contracts as well.
  • FedRAMP: inherited at the infrastructure level, accurately described. The LAIRA cloud platform runs on commercial AWS US regions. The AWS services beneath it carry FedRAMP Moderate authorization at the infrastructure level. Airwai does not hold its own FedRAMP authorization and does not claim one. Workloads that require FedRAMP High or Department of War impact-level handling are deployed via AWS GovCloud (US) under a separate Order Form and architecture review.
  • No claimed certification we do not hold. This is also a contractual commitment in our DPA: we update this page as attestations are achieved, and our contracts make no representation beyond what is stated here.

Data residency

United States,
full stop.

  • All customer data is stored and processed in AWS regions located in the United States. Airwai does not transfer customer data outside the United States.
  • Airwai support and engineering personnel access customer data only from within the United States.

Encryption, access control, and identity

Encrypted in transit,
encrypted at rest.

  • In transit: TLS 1.2 or higher for all data moving between field devices, the LAIRA platform, and your endpoints, including your ArcGIS environment.
  • At rest: AES-256 encryption for all customer data, with encryption keys managed in AWS Key Management Service (KMS).
  • Tenant isolation: logical tenant isolation in the database tier, including row-level security.
  • Single sign-on: SAML 2.0 and OIDC federation for your identity provider, alongside native authentication with MFA.
  • Airwai-side access: least-privilege access for Airwai personnel; production access is restricted to named engineers, logged, and reviewed; MFA is required for all Airwai administrative access. You administer your own users and roles in the platform.

Platform and field architecture

The field hardware
never touches your network.

  • Segregated AWS accounts for production, logging, and audit functions, with centralized log archiving and infrastructure-as-code change management.
  • Private-subnet, firewalled database tiers; vulnerability management with prioritized remediation.
Field hardware (Rig deployments)

The LAIRA Rig uplinks over its own cellular connection and is air-gapped from customer IT. Data is encrypted in transit from the device. There is no agent to install, no firewall exception to request, and no VPN into your environment. For many airport and agency IT teams, this is the answer to the first question they ask.

Your data, your outcomes

The data-rights summary.

This summarizes the data terms in our agreements (the DPA and the governing subscription agreement). Plain version:

  • You receive the outcomes for your assets. Detections, condition assessments, reports, and ArcGIS layers for your sites are delivered to you through the LAIRA dashboard and the Airwai API, and you can export them at any time during your term.
  • We never sell your data, and we never process it for third-party advertising.
  • Airwai retains anonymized and aggregated condition and outcome data. We keep the right to use de-identified, aggregated infrastructure condition data (distress types, severities, deterioration rates) and outcome data (treatments applied, performance over time) to train models, benchmark, and improve the product. De-identified data can never identify you, any specific site or facility, or any person, and we commit contractually not to attempt re-identification. This is how every customer's platform gets better; it is in every Airwai contract.
  • Outcomes, not labels. Airwai delivers outcomes via the dashboard and API. We do not deliver labeled training datasets, model weights, or pixel-level scoring internals to any customer or partner. The platform and models remain Airwai's.
  • Screening and compliance are different things, and we label them. Upload and Capture outputs are screening-grade and carry the label "Screening — not for compliance use." Only outputs of the LAIRA Rig are produced to compliance-grade specifications (ASTM D6433 / D5340, FAA Part 139, UFC 3-260-04, 23 CFR 490, PROWAG). We put this on our pricing page, in our contracts, and on every report, because a vendor that overstates evidence grade is a vendor you should not trust with infrastructure data.
  • Incidental imagery. Field imagery can incidentally capture people, vehicles, or plates. The platform is not designed for, and customers agree not to submit, health, payment-card, biometric, or other special-category personal data.

Data return and deletion

What happens
at contract end.

  • During your term: export your outputs and reports at any time via the dashboard and API, in supported formats including ArcGIS-compatible export.
  • At contract end: your dashboard and API access continue in read/export-only mode for 60 days.
  • After the export window: Airwai deletes or de-identifies your customer data within 90 days, except de-identified data retained under the data-rights terms above, data in routine backups (overwritten in the ordinary course), and anything law requires us to keep. Written confirmation of deletion is available on request.
  • Rig hardware: Rig-as-Service hardware is Airwai-owned. At contract end we securely wipe customer data from recovered devices as part of decommissioning.

Incident response

Notice within 72 hours
of confirmation.

If we confirm a security incident affecting your data, we notify you without undue delay, and in any event within 72 hours of confirmation, with the nature and scope of the incident, the data categories affected, the measures taken or planned, and a contact point, supplemented as the investigation develops. The full mechanics are in the DPA.

Availability and support

99.5% monthly uptime,
credit-backed.

The LAIRA cloud platform carries a 99.5% monthly uptime commitment, measured by automated synthetic monitoring, with a published service-credit schedule, defined support severities and response targets, and a chronic-failure termination right. Rig-as-Service subscriptions additionally carry a hardware-availability commitment (remote diagnosis within one business day; repair-or-replace shipment targets; out-of-service credits). The SLA is the complete commitment.
