How buyers procure LAIRA

Procurement vehicles, named.

A federal KO doesn’t buy software; they execute a contract under a buying authority. Airwai is structured to be bought under the vehicles your contracting officer already knows how to run.

Commercial ItemFAR Part 12 · subpart 12.207
Direct subscription purchase under commercial-item rules. Fastest path. NDAA-compliant by construction; covered-foreign-technology vetted under §889. No GSA schedule required.
Simplified AcquisitionFAR Part 13 · micro & SAP
Micro-purchase up to $15K (P-card), SAP up to $250K. Useful for first install, partial-year scope, or single-installation procurement before a multi-year vehicle is in place.
SBIR Phase III15 U.S.C. §638
Sole-source continuation pathway. Available for qualifying agencies with prior SBIR work. Carries the SBIR data-rights protection through deployment.
Multi-year serviceFAR 17.1 · option years
Annual base year with option years. Standard structure for ongoing customers — runs the subscription on a procurement vehicle the agency already understands.
State DOT purchaseState procurement code
Direct purchase against pavement-management line items. Subscription billable as a service under the operations side of the capital + ops budget.
AEC subcontractPrime → sub task
Assigned task under an AEC prime’s contract with the end customer. Used where the prime owns the relationship and Airwai delivers the data layer.

Procurement & contracting

What contracting officers ask.

What does a typical contract look like?

Annual base year with option years. Multi-year service-contract structure that rolls under FAR Part 12 commercial-item terms. The contract covers platform, on-site install, support, and ongoing updates. Re-audit cadence is set in the SOW.

How does the install actually work?

Airwai engineers deploy the rig on your operations vehicle on-site. One day per vehicle. The rig is sensor-agnostic and bolts to standard roof or bed mounting points. No vehicle retrofit, no body modification, no permanent attachment required.

Who owns the data?

You do. Outputs publish to your ArcGIS feature service, in your tenant, on your storage. Airwai retains no copy of customer data without explicit written authorization. SBIR-protected data rights apply where contracts are executed under SBIR Phase III.

Is the supply chain NDAA-compliant?

Yes. COTS hardware stack vetted against §889 covered-foreign-technology restrictions. No prohibited equipment, period. Component-level supply chain documentation available under NDA on request.

Who else is deployed?

San Bernardino International Airport — the customer story is public and was featured by Esri. Additional deployments are confirmed under NDA and available on request during the scope call.

Can we run a validation engagement before signing the full contract?

Yes — if your acquisition rules require it. A 30-day scoped engagement on one corridor, one apron, or one parking lot can be executed under SAP / P-card thresholds, with the engagement fee credited toward the Year 1 contract value when the customer moves to the full agreement. Most customers buy directly.

What about insurance, indemnification, and standard reps?

Commercial general liability, errors & omissions, and cyber coverage in place. Airwai accepts standard federal acquisition reps and certifications. Mutual indemnification on commercial contracts; federal reps standard under FAR 52.

What happens at renewal?

Annual renewal at the option-year structure agreed in the base contract. Scope adjustments (more vehicles, more surfaces, expanded cadence) go through a written contract mod — not a re-negotiation. Customers running multi-year agreements see no per-renewal contracting drag.
