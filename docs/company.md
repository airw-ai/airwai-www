## How we operate — confident, specific, citation-forward

### 01 · Evidence over assertion

**Name the standard. Name the sensor. Name the resolution.** Every measurement cites the section it implements (ASTM D6433 §X1.14, 23 CFR Part 490, UFC 3-260). If a claim doesn't terminate at a public source, it doesn't ship.

### 02 · Decisive, not severe

**Software for the engineer responsible when the audit lands.** Generous space, decisive answers, no hand-waving. The brand carries institutional weight without trading away clarity. We build for the buyer who has to defend the deliverable in writing.

### 03 · Contrarian where earned

**ARAN-class incumbents are roadway-only. The default is paper.** Sidewalk, curb-ramp, and marking inspection is a separate procurement today. LAIRA collapses three procurements into one — and produces output ArcGIS-native, not a PDF.

## Built for secure sites from the ground up

Secure-site customers don't have the luxury of trial-and-error procurement. Airwai is built to clear federal acquisition review on its first pass — supply chain, data tenancy, and operational posture.

- **NDAA-compliant COTS hardware stack** vetted against §889 covered-foreign-technology restrictions. No prohibited equipment, period.
- **Edge-first inference.** Detection runs on-rig at the airfield. No internet dependency during ingest, no telemetry off-site without operator authorization.
- **Customer-owned data.** Outputs publish to your ArcGIS feature service, your tenant, your storage. Airwai is the platform — the data is yours.
- **Citation chain.** Every detection traces to a standard section and a sensor measurement. Auditors and contracting officers can verify any claim end-to-end.
- **US-based team.** Delaware C-Corp, California-headquartered, US-citizen team for any work touching CUI or controlled-installation data.

## Intelligence for the infrastructure that matters

We operate at airfields, military installations, and roadway networks that carry national-level operations. The condition of that infrastructure is a national-security data layer — not a paperwork artifact.

Department of War installations alone manage thousands of miles of runway, taxiway, apron, and access pavement. The current default is hand-walked inspections on a slow cadence, paper records, and reactive maintenance. We're building the platform that turns continuous-scan data into decisions, in the workflow operators already use.

The same pattern carries through to FAA Part 139 airports, state DOTs, and AEC primes who own the pre-design baseline. **One platform, sensor-agnostic, citation-chained, audit-ready by construction.**
